Udkast på engelsk (spillets kildesprog). Punkter er delt i **sandt i dag** og **planlagt**; planlagte punkter må ikke stå på en offentlig side, før de er bygget.

## Short description (EN)

Defend the last world tree. Build a deck that can take root. A turn-based roguelike deckbuilder where three floors of a living tree stand between the ash and its turquoise Heart.

## Long description (EN)

The ash is climbing. Each turn new enemies arrive on the Roots, fight your creatures floor by floor and climb towards the Crown. Whatever gets past the Crown strikes the Heart of the last world tree.

Choose your champion — the Barkwarden, a wall of bark and armor, or the Spore Sage, who poisons the ash before it climbs — and play cards onto the Roots, the Trunk and the Crown. A clear forecast shows exactly what ending the turn will do, so every loss is a decision you can learn from.

Between battles you choose your path: camps to upgrade a card, encounters, card rewards or removals. Mutations reshape the tree itself, relics bend the rules, and your champion grows along one of two paths. Five battles and the Ash Crown stand between you and victory.

## Feature bullets

**True today (in the current build):**
- Turn-based battles on three floors (Roots, Trunk, Crown) with a front line next to the trunk.
- An exact end-of-turn forecast that uses the same rules as the battle.
- Two champions (Barkwarden, Spore Sage), 24 cards, 8 relics, 4 tree mutations.
- Five battles and a boss with its own rules (the Ash Crown).
- A branching route with camps, card rewards, encounters and card removal.
- Card upgrades and champion paths with before/after numbers.
- Armor, Poison, Thorns and Growth, always shown as symbol + number.
- English and Danish.
- Text size, reduced motion, animation speed and volume settings.
- Mouse, touch, keyboard and gamepad (simulated in tests; real controller not yet tested).
- Desktop (Windows, Linux) and web exports exist; portrait phone layout.
- Save after every action; continue exactly where you left off.

**Planned (not in the build yet — do not publish as features):**
- Final 3D art and animation (Synty Studios, PolyPerfect, Mixamo; pending import and licence check). Today all 3D is procedural placeholder geometry.
- Final sound and music (today: procedural placeholders).
- Native mobile builds and testing on real phones/tablets.
- Screen-reader support.
